How this simulation is solved

Honeycomb wall = Z₂ rupture (Be2, T3)

Each hexagonal cell wall is a CRR agent. Workers visit; each visit deposits L wax-units of coherence onto the wall. When C·Ω = 1 (Ω = 1/π), the wall closes — a Z₂ rupture event. The 6-fold local angular constraint is the SO(2) regulator. Predicted CV across cells: 1/(2π) ≈ 0.159. Measured live in the metric strip.

Empirical anchor: Hepburn et al. PNAS 2010, fresh comb 88 ± 10 μm → CV 0.114; Session-8 result: Be2 → T3.

Cell tilt 13° = C* − Ω geodesic

Brood cells tilt upward by ~13°. CRR identifies this as the beauty-function peak C* − Ω of the wax/honey-mass surface-tension geodesic — the optimum on the comb-mass / wall-strength tradeoff (Bauer & Bienefeld 2020).

Worker flight = SO(2) phase observable

Worker bees trace SO(2) loops (forage → return). Heading uses the sun-azimuth / polarisation-pattern compass. Predicted angular CV under Class A autonomous: 1/(4π); waggle dance is empirically Class B regulated — bees are more precise than the autonomous baseline (selection for accurate communication; Be1).

Drone = Z₂ rupture without SO(2) regulator (M23)

Drone life is mating-or-die. After successful mating, the drone's CRR coherence ruptures terminally. This is the canonical M23 picture (radiation paper): a Z₂ rupture system with no closed SO(2) cycle to return to. Drone inter-mating-attempt CV → 1.

Queen = Z₂ × SO(2) compound

Queen mating flight: SO(2) high-altitude orbit through drone-congregation airspace; each mating with a drone is a Z₂ rupture event (12–20× per nuptial flight). Egg-laying back in the hive: SO(2) cycle through cells × Z₂ egg-rupture per cell. Haplodiploid switch: fertilised egg → diploid worker/queen (32 chromosomes); unfertilised → haploid drone (16). The ×2 chromosome doubling at fertilisation is the canonical Z₂:SO(2) topological ratio made cellular.

Sensory hierarchy (geometry of the senses)

Compound eye: 4 SO(2) relays at ~2 mm interocular separation. Antennae: 2 SO(2) relays at ~1 mm. Per geometry_of_the_senses.pdf: paired-Z₂-aperture separation predicts SO(2) integration depth via n + 1/n. Confirmed in bees (Be4, Be5; Session 8).

Circadian = Class B regulated

Honeybee free-run period CV ≈ 0.022 in DD (well below 1/(4π) = 0.080). Class B. The bee circadian clock is tightly entrained, joining cyanobacteria, Drosophila, and mouse SCN as Session-7 / Be6 confirmed Class B exemplars.
