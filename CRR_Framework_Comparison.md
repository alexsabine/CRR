## Conclusion

CRR occupies a unique position in the theoretical landscape:

1. **Highest discontinuity focus** among memory-aware frameworks
2. **Highest memory integration** among discontinuity-focused frameworks
3. **Strongest cross-domain mathematical coverage** (24+ domains)
4. **Explicit philosophical depth** connecting to contemplative traditions
5. **Designed for FEP complementarity** rather than competition

**Primary Limitation:** Still early in validation cycle compared to mature frameworks (DST, NET).

**Key Contribution:** Provides the "missing piece" for frameworks like FEP that handle continuous dynamics well but lack explicit discontinuous transition mechanisms with memory-weighted recovery.
