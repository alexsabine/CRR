# Be9: csd allele frequency distribution shows CV ≈ 1 (M23 / Class C)

## Canonical statement

csd is maintained under negative-frequency-dependent selection (rare alleles favoured). The resulting frequency distribution is the population-genetic analogue of a memoryless Z₂-without-SO(2) process: each generation samples alleles independently of accumulated history. CRR M23 predicts CV ≈ 1.
