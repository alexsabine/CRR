# B13: Drosophila wing-beat period CV equals 1/(4π)

## Canonical statement

Drosophila wing-beat is a continuous flight-motor SO(2) cycle (~200 Hz). CV across cycles tests SO(2).

## Domain(s)

B, T

## Source in CRR canon

Pre-registered Session 7. Data target: Published high-speed wing-beat tracking studies (Lehmann, Dickinson, Fry, Tu).
