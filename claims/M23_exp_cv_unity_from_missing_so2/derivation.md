# M23 — Derivation

## Setup

Let the system have Z₂ rupture topology (Bernoulli draw at
threshold C·Ω = 1) but **no** SO(2) regulatory substrate. By
Convention C2, the absence of SO(2) means there is no continuous
phase manifold whose geodesic governs Ω — equivalently, there is
no closed-cycle accumulation between ruptures. This is the
"memoryless" condition.

## Identity (Sabine 2026, radioactive paper §3)

CRR baseline Z₂ prediction:

    CV_{Z₂} = Ω/2 = 1/(2π).

Geodesic extent of the *absent* SO(2) substrate (the manifold the
system would have if it were rotationally regulated):

    C*_{SO(2)} = 2π.

The exponential CV is the product:

    CV_exp = CV_{Z₂} × C*_{SO(2)} = (1/(2π)) × 2π = 1.

## Asymmetry

The reverse product `CV_{SO(2)} × C*_{Z₂} = (1/(4π)) × π = 1/4`
does NOT equal 1. The relationship is structurally one-sided:
*the Z₂ inflates by the missing SO(2) extent*, never the other way
round. This matches the physical asymmetry that a Z₂ rupture
requires an SO(2) substrate to gate timing, but a continuous
rotation can exist without producing binary events.

## Discrete distribution check

The geometric distribution (discrete memoryless) has CV = √(1−p)/p,
which varies continuously with `p` and contains no clean 2π factor.
This rules out coincidence: the 2π factor appears **only** on the
continuous side, where SO(2) could have existed as a substrate but
does not.

## Verification (from the paper)

- Analytical: identity is exact.
- Monte Carlo: 100 trials × 10⁶ exponential samples → CV = 1.000099 ± 0.000953.
- Literature search (Apr 2026): no prior derivation identified.
