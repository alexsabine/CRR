# P15 — Alkali D2-line CV converges to f = 2 across the full series Li → Fr

## Canonical statement

Per F_Structure.pdf (Sabine 2025) §6–§7 and Table 3, alkali metals
in their primary resonance ns → np transition are predicted to
satisfy

    CV = α³ / (4π · f),    f_pred = 2.

This f-value is set by the alkali-group structure (single
unpaired ns valence electron, Δl = ±1 dipole transition into np),
and is independent of n. The F-Structure paper validates this
against NIST data for K, Rb, Cs (mean f = 1.97, 1.5% error).

P15 extends the test to **Francium** — explicitly named as a
future prediction in F_Structure §11.2:

> "(i) Francium D-line CV = α³/(8π) ≈ 1.55 × 10⁻⁸, following the
> alkali convergence."

— and to **Lithium and Sodium**, which F_Structure §11.1 flags as
"light paired s-electron" deviants where electron–electron
correlation is strong. Including them tests how sharply the
deviant cluster separates from the convergent cluster.
