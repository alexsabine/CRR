# P8: Pulsar glitch inter-arrival CV equals 1/(2π)

## Canonical statement

Pulsar glitches are sudden spin-up events on a continuously
spinning-down neutron star. Each glitch is a Z₂ rupture (the star
either has glitched or has not at any instant; the avalanche is
Bernoulli-class at threshold). Per Convention C1 (rupture is Z₂
by construction), the inter-glitch-interval CV is

    CV = Ω/2 = 1/(2π) ≈ 0.1592.

## Domain(s)

P (physical), T (temporal)

## Source in CRR canon

Avalanche / rupture systems are canonically Z₂ in the
rupture-topology framework (`notes/rupture_topology.md`).
Glitch-rich pulsars (Vela, Crab) provide multi-decade catalogues.
