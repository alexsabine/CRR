# Be3: Drone-cell to worker-cell linear ratio = 4/3 ± 5%

## Canonical statement

Worker (~5.2 mm) and drone (~6.9 mm) cell diameters give a ratio that maps onto the n+1/n CRR construction at n=3 (equivalently, 4/3 = 1 + 1/3). The brood-comb dimorphism encodes a discrete CRR ratio.

## Domain(s)

B, M

## Source

Pre-reg test against published canonical cell-diameter measurements (Seeley; Winston; Hepburn).
