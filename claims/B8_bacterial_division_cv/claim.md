# B8: Single-cell bacterial generation-time CV equals 1/(2π)

## Canonical statement

Bacterial division is a **Z₂ rupture event** (the cell either has
divided or has not at any given moment; the division operator is
Bernoulli(1/2) at the rupture threshold under the C·Ω = 1
condition). Per the canonical CRR formulation (`CAMPAIGN.md`
PART I, Convention C1 in `notes/conventions.md`):

> All ruptures are Z₂ by construction.

For a Z₂-rupture-dominated process the generation-time CV is

    CV = Ω/2 = 1/(2π) ≈ 0.1592

This is parameter-free; no fitted constants enter.

## Why a B8 pre-registration is meaningful

Three reasons:

1. **Z₂ vs SO(2) discrimination.** Eukaryotic cell-cycle CVs
   (yeast, mammalian) cluster around 0.20–0.40 — *higher* than
   1/(2π) — consistent with regulatory load (Class B). Bacterial
   division is the cleanest Z₂ test because the regulatory
   apparatus is minimal.
2. **Independent published data.** Single-cell mother-machine and
   microfluidic experiments (Wang et al. 2010; Stewart et al.
   2005; Taheri-Araghi et al. 2015; Susman et al. 2018) provide
   peer-reviewed cohort statistics that pre-date this prediction.
3. **Sharp falsifier.** The CRR Z₂ prediction CV ≈ 0.159 is
   distinguishable from the SO(2) prediction CV ≈ 0.080 (factor
   of 2) and from regulated/Class-B CVs > 0.20.
