# M22: Lie-group CV generalisation

## Canonical statement

For any compact connected Lie group G acting as the continual
memory-bearing manifold under Z₂ rupture, with bi-invariant
Riemannian metric and closed-geodesic length φ_G, the canonical CRR
coefficient of variation is

    CV_G = 1 / (2 · φ_G).

This subsumes the canonical Z₂ and SO(2) cases (which become
specific values φ_Z₂ = π and φ_SO(2) = 2π) and predicts CV values
for other compact Lie groups including SU(2), SO(3), T^n, SU(3).

## Domain(s)

M, T

## Source in CRR canon

This claim is the natural generalisation of M1, M2, M15 under the
rupture-topology framework formalised in `notes/rupture_topology.md`
(Session 2 resolution). It is *not* explicitly stated in the
canonical brief but is implicit in the brief's "ALL RUPTURES ARE Z₂"
assertion together with the SO(2) phase-manifold identification.

The user-prompted hypothesis (that the framework "stretches to any
Lie group or geometric structure where Z₂ is the discrete cut and
the phase / SO(2) structure is the continual memory-bearing
manifold") is added to the formal claim set as M22.
