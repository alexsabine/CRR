# B9: Healthy resting respiratory cycle CV equals 1/(4π)

## Canonical statement

The respiratory cycle in healthy humans at rest is a continuous
cyclic motor program (inspire → expire → inspire → ...). Its
internal degree of freedom traces a closed orbit on a continuous
phase manifold. Per the canonical CRR formulation (`CAMPAIGN.md`
PART I) the appropriate phase manifold is **SO(2)** (the cycle is
parametrised by a continuous phase φ ∈ [0, 2π) returning to its
starting state):

    φ_{SO(2)} = 2π
    Ω_{SO(2)} = 1/(2π)
    CV        = Ω/2 = 1/(4π) ≈ 0.0796

This is parameter-free.

## Why a B9 pre-registration is meaningful

1. **SO(2) test on a system distinct from cardiac.** B7 and B2
   are HRV-focused (cardiac SO(2)); B9 tests SO(2) on a different
   physiological system, providing an independent SO(2) datum.
2. **Discrimination between SO(2) and Z₂.** Healthy resting
   respiration should give CV ≈ 0.0796, distinguishable by a
   factor of 2 from the Z₂ band 1/(2π) ≈ 0.1592.
3. **Independent published cohort data.** Polysomnographic studies
   and resting-state metabolic studies (PubMed-indexed) routinely
   report respiratory rate variability. The cohorts pre-date this
   prediction.
