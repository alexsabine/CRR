Coherence: Energy Accumulation

Ocean waves accumulate energy through wind friction transferring momentum to water particles. In deep water, particles move in circular orbits, gradually building coherence as energy integrates over time. The longer and stronger the wind blows, the more coherent the wave field becomes.

Mathematical Form:
$$C(x,t) = \int_0^t L(x,\tau) \, d\tau$$

Where \(L(x,\tau)\) is the memory density representing energy input rate from wind stress and orbital motion.

Physical Interpretation: As waves propagate, each water parcel "remembers" the energy it has received. Coherence increases with wave amplitude and steepness, capturing the system's approach toward an unstable critical threshold.

Rupture: Wave Breaking

When waves enter shallow water or become too steep, they undergo rupture - a discontinuous transition marked by the Dirac delta function δ(t-t₀). This occurs when:

  • Wave steepness exceeds the critical ratio H/λ > 1/7 (height/wavelength)
  • Wave height exceeds 78% of water depth in shallow regions
  • Accumulated coherence crosses the rupture threshold
Rupture Event:
$$\delta(t - t_0) = \begin{cases} \infty & \text{if } C(x,t) \geq C_{\text{critical}} \text{ at } t = t_0 \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

The Dirac delta marks the instantaneous moment of breaking, releasing accumulated energy as turbulent foam.

Physical Process: Breaking waves violently reorganize the water column. Circular orbits collapse into forward-moving turbulence, dissipating energy and creating whitecaps and foam patterns.

Regeneration: Wave Recovery and New Pattern Formation

After breaking, the ocean doesn't simply dissipate - it regenerates. Memory of past wave patterns (stored in coherence field) guides the reformation of new waves. The wash from broken waves reorganizes into secondary wave trains.

Regeneration Operator:
$$R[\chi](x,t) = \int_0^t \phi(x,\tau) \cdot e^{C(x,\tau)/\Omega} \cdot \Theta(t-\tau) \, d\tau$$

Where φ represents historical wave fields, \(e^{C/\Omega}\) exponentially weights high-coherence states, and Θ (Heaviside) ensures causality.

Observed Behavior: Broken wave energy doesn't vanish - it redistributes. The regeneration operator captures how surf zones maintain rhythmic patterns despite continuous breaking, with Ω (CRR temperature) controlling the memory timescale.

Key Insight:

Ocean waves are not memoryless! CRR reveals that wave breaking is a phase transition where the system's accumulated history (coherence) dictates when and how breaking occurs. Post-rupture regeneration explains why beaches have consistent wave patterns even though each wave is unique - the system remembers and reconstructs based on past energy distributions.
