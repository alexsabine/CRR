## Cross-domain coherence

A noteworthy property: CRR has T2+ claims in **all four domains**.
Most candidate frameworks reach in only one or two. CRR's
cross-domain reach is structurally distinctive — even where the
tier count is modest, the *scope* of the framework is broad.

This may be a real feature (the framework genuinely captures
cross-domain process structure), or it may be an artefact of
CRR's deliberate cross-domain framing. The campaign cannot
adjudicate. It records the cross-domain T2+ count (10 across 4
domains) as observed.
