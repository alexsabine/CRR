# Philosophical assessment framework

This document defines the framework for assessing CRR's
philosophical and phenomenological claims (Ph1–Ph7) per the
Session 5 mandate.

## Three modes of interpretation

Every philosophical-CRR identification is assessed against three
distinct modes of interpretation. The modes are *not* a tier
ladder; they are *kinds* of relationship between formalism and
philosophy. A given claim might be exact in one direction and
metaphorical in another.

### M — Metaphorical

CRR's formalism *resembles* the philosophical claim by analogy.
The math is suggestive and the intuitions align, but the formal
elements do not formally instantiate the philosophical content;
they merely echo it.

**Indicator:** removing the philosophical interpretation does not
change the formalism's mathematical content. The philosophy is
"poetry on top of equations."

**Examples elsewhere:** "the Schrödinger equation describes the
quantum waltz of reality" — the waltz adds nothing formally.

**Tier ceiling for purely-metaphorical CRR claims:** T1 (the
philosophical statement is a *gesture* at meaning, not a
verifiable claim).

### S — Structural

CRR's formalism reproduces the **relational structure** of the
philosophical claim — the same set of conceptual relationships
holds between formal elements as between philosophical concepts.
The mapping is *not* one-to-one in interpretation; remainders
exist on both sides.

**Indicator:** there is a partial-isomorphism between the
philosophical concept-graph and the CRR formal element-graph.
Operations that are valid in one are valid in the other under the
mapping.

**Tier ceiling for structurally-reconstructive CRR claims:**
T2-equivalent (reconstruction with remainder; per CAMPAIGN.md
PART III philosophical pathway).

### E — Exact

CRR's formalism reconstructs the philosophical claim *without
remainder*. Every element of the philosophical claim has a formal
counterpart, and every formal element corresponds to something in
the philosophical claim. The formalism *is* the philosophy,
mathematicised.

**Indicator:** any operation specifiable in the philosophical
claim is specifiable in CRR, and vice versa. The two are mutually
translatable in full.

**Tier ceiling for exactly-reconstructive CRR claims that also
predict a phenomenological regularity not in the original
philosophical source:** T3-equivalent.

## Tier ladder for philosophical claims

Per CAMPAIGN.md PART III philosophical pathway:

- **T1 (philosophical):** the claim has explicit assumptions and
  an inference pattern, even if the inference is non-deductive.
  All listed Ph claims have this floor.

- **T2-equivalent (philosophical):** the CRR formalism
  *reconstructs the philosophical claim's structural commitments
  without remainder* on the formal side. (Note: "without remainder
  on the formal side" is more relaxed than the M/S/E "exact" tier
  — we ask only that everything in the philosophy that can be
  formalised *is* formalised; not that all CRR formal structure
  must have philosophical interpretation.)

- **T3-equivalent (philosophical):** the formalism predicts a
  phenomenological regularity not stated in the original
  philosophical source. (E.g., a specific quantitative band on
  some experimental measurement.)

- **T4-equivalent (philosophical):** independent philosophers /
  phenomenologists have engaged with the CRR reconstruction in
  published work.

A claim can be *structural* in M/S/E but only *T1* in tier — if
the structural reconstruction has too much remainder. The two
axes are not strictly aligned.

## Honesty requirements (per CAMPAIGN.md)

1. Philosophical claims that remain at T2-equivalent after this
   session should not be presented as theory.
2. Rhetorical resonance is not evidence. "CRR captures the soul
   of Whiteheadian becoming" — without a structural argument, this
   is metaphorical.
3. Where CRR commits to controversial metaphysical positions
   (presentism, pansychism, etc.), the commitment is recorded
   transparently.
4. Where independent philosopher engagement does not exist (likely
   for most Ph claims as of 2026), the absence is recorded
   honestly.

## What we do NOT do

- We do not "promote" Ph claims to T2-equivalent on the basis of
  the philosophy resembling CRR's formalism *in a hand-waving
  sense*. The structural mapping must be specifiable.
- We do not extend CRR formalism beyond its mathematical content
  to fit philosophical claims. The math is fixed; the question is
  whether the philosophy maps onto it.
- We do not under-assess. Where CRR genuinely reconstructs
  philosophical structure, we say so.

## Per-claim assessment template

Each Ph<n>_assessment.md addresses, in order:

1. **The philosophical claim** in its strongest canonical form
   (with citations to primary sources).
2. **The CRR-side formal mapping** — which mathematical objects
   are claimed to correspond.
3. **Metaphorical / Structural / Exact assessment** — which mode
   does the mapping achieve, and where do remainders sit?
4. **Phenomenological predictions** — what testable consequences
   (if any) flow from the reconstruction that are not stated in
   the original philosophical source?
5. **Independent engagement** — has any independent philosopher
   engaged with this CRR reconstruction in publication?
6. **Tier assessment** under the philosophical pathway.
7. **Applied usefulness for 2026 and beyond** — operational
   contexts where the philosophical-formal bridge has consequences.

## Outputs from Session 5

Seven per-claim assessment files (one per Ph1–Ph7) plus this
framework document, plus tier file updates and classification-
table refresh. Each per-claim file is committed alongside its
tier promotion or non-promotion.
