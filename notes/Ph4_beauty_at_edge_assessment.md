# Ph4 — Assessment: Beauty / agency at C* − Ω (the edge)

## The philosophical claim

The CRR beauty function B(C) = exp(C/Ω)·(C* − C) attains its
maximum at C = C* − Ω — *one Ω-step before the rupture
threshold*. The philosophical reading:

- **Aesthetic:** beauty is not at completion (C = C*) nor at
  origin (C = 0), but at the edge of becoming.
- **Agential / ethical:** agency is exercised at C* − Ω, where
  the system is maximally *poised* — accumulated coherence is
  high, but the Ω-budget for further accumulation remains.
- **Existential:** life is most "alive" at the edge, not at the
  rupture itself.

This connects to a network of established edge-of-chaos /
optimal-poise hypotheses:
- **Edge of chaos** (Langton 1990; Kauffman *Origins of Order*
  1993; Packard 1988): adaptive computation peaks at
  λ ≈ 0.5-criticality.
- **Critical brain hypothesis** (Beggs & Plenz 2003; Chialvo
  2010): healthy neural dynamics sits at criticality.
- **Optimal foraging / explore-exploit** (Charnov 1976; Bellman
  1957): rational agents balance exploration and exploitation —
  the edge between rigid-stay and chaotic-search.
- **Aesthetic theories of "tension and release"** (Meyer 1956
  *Emotion and Meaning in Music*; Huron 2006 *Sweet
  Anticipation*): aesthetic peak before resolution, not at it.

## Metaphorical / Structural / Exact assessment

**Mixed: the mathematical claim is exact (M12 is T1-derived);
the *philosophical interpretation* is at most structural and
arguably metaphorical.**

What is *exact*:
- The location of the maximum, C* − Ω, is provable calculus.
- The functional form of B(C) is the canonical CRR proposal.
- The connection to canonical edge-of-chaos / criticality
  literature is structurally legible.

What is *structural*:
- Identifying B's maximum with "where agency lives" or "where
  beauty is" is a *philosophical labelling* of the mathematical
  fact. Different aesthetic / agency theories can endorse this
  labelling; the labelling itself is not implied by the math.
- The structural match with edge-of-chaos hypotheses is genuine:
  *if* one accepts that adaptive computation peaks at the edge
  of phase transitions, *then* CRR's C* − Ω location is a
  candidate formalisation of that intuition.

What is *metaphorical*:
- "Beauty" as an aesthetic-philosophical category includes
  considerations (Kantian disinterestedness, Hegelian
  Idea-realisation, Adornian negativity, etc.) that no
  single-variable maximum can capture.
- "Agency" as a philosophical category includes free-will,
  intentionality, normativity, etc. — none implied by an extremum
  of B(C).

## Phenomenological predictions

The C* − Ω edge prediction is **quantitative**, not just
philosophical:

- **Neural-criticality measurements:** M/EEG-derived
  "criticality indices" (avalanche distributions, branching
  parameter; Beggs-Plenz tradition) should peak when the
  *coherence-to-saturation* fraction is (C* − Ω)/C* — i.e., a
  CRR-specific prediction in terms of the system's saturation
  point.
- **Optimal-foraging:** explore-exploit transitions should occur
  at the C* − Ω boundary in foraging-rate variables; testable in
  cognitive-neuroscience explore-exploit paradigms (Daw et al.
  2006; subsequent meta-analyses).
- **Aesthetic-peak timing in music:** the "moment of maximum
  expectation tension" (Huron's prediction-violation-resolution
  arc) should sit at C* − Ω in a coherence-of-expectation
  variable. Computationally testable on music-information-
  retrieval datasets.
- **Sport-performance "flow":** Csikszentmihalyi's flow state
  is structurally edge-poised (skill matched to challenge);
  CRR predicts flow's coherence statistics peak at C* − Ω.

These are non-trivial CRR-specific predictions not stated in
canonical aesthetic / agency literature.

## Tier assessment

**T2-equivalent (philosophical pathway).**

Justification: the structural mapping between CRR's
B-maximum-at-C*−Ω and the family of edge-of-chaos / critical-
poise / explore-exploit / aesthetic-tension theories is
genuine and specifiable. The CRR formula is mathematically
exact (M12 derivation T1); the *philosophical framing* is
structural — strongest where edge-of-chaos research is
quantitatively testable.

T3-equivalent **not** awarded: while several phenomenological
predictions are quantitatively specifiable (criticality-index
peak, foraging-transition, aesthetic-peak timing), no
empirical reconstruction has demonstrated CRR's C*−Ω
prediction *as a test outperforming* the neighbouring
frameworks. T3 would require a pre-registered prediction
where CRR's *specific* edge location (C*−Ω) wins where
generic-criticality losses.

T4-equivalent **not** awarded: no independent engagement.
