# Ph3 — Assessment: δ(now) marks the ontological present

## The philosophical claim

The Dirac delta δ(now) — the instant at which C·Ω = 1 — marks an
**ontological present**: not merely an epistemic/perspectival
present, but a metaphysically privileged moment at which past is
metabolised into future.

This commits CRR to a position in the long-running philosophy-of-
time debate:

- **Eternalism / B-theory** (McTaggart's B-series; Russell;
  Mellor): time is a four-dimensional manifold; "now" is an
  indexical with no special metaphysical status.
- **Presentism / A-theory** (McTaggart's A-series; Bigelow,
  Crisp): only the present moment is real; past and future do
  not exist.
- **Growing-block / "moving spotlight"** (Broad 1923; Cameron
  2015): past is real, future is not (or vice versa).

CRR's δ(now) — singled out structurally as the rupture instant
where the system metabolises past coherence into regenerated
future — leans toward an **A-theory / presentist or growing-
block** position.

## CRR-side formal mapping

- **δ(now)**: scale-invariant Dirac delta marking the rupture
  instant.
- **C·Ω = 1**: the threshold condition.
- **Asymmetry**: Heaviside Θ(t−τ) makes past *causally* prior to
  future, distinct from the eternalist symmetric block.

## Metaphorical / Structural / Exact assessment

**Structural — with a strong metaphysical commitment that
CAMPAIGN.md PART III honesty requires us to flag.**

What CRR captures:
- **Privileged structural role for "now"** ✓: δ(now) is not just
  a label but a structurally-distinguished moment in the
  formalism.
- **Past-future asymmetry** ✓: built into the formalism via Θ.
- **Discrete becoming** ✓: δ as a localised event, not a
  continuous indexical.

What CRR does NOT capture:
- **The metaphysical *experience* of present-ness:** CRR gives
  δ a *mathematical* role; it does not explain why δ "feels
  present" to a conscious observer. (Connects to Ph6.)
- **Compatibility with relativistic spacetime:** in special
  relativity, simultaneity is frame-dependent. CRR's δ(now)
  appears to commit to a universal "now" — which is *prima
  facie* in tension with relativity. The CRR-FEP correspondence
  (CAMPAIGN.md PART I) gestures at a per-system δ(now) that is
  Markov-blanket-relative, but the brief does not fully resolve
  the relativistic-simultaneity question.

## Phenomenological predictions

Phenomenological consequences flowing from Ph3:

- **The "specious present"** of psychophysics (William James 1890;
  Le Poidevin 2007) — the experienced "now" has duration ~50-
  500 ms, not zero — could be reframed as the *neighbourhood* of
  CRR's δ in phenomenological time. CRR predicts the specious
  present is centred on rupture events, which is in principle
  testable (M/EEG correlates of rupture-event timing vs reported
  "now").
- **Predicted rupture-onset jitter:** if δ(now) is real, the
  CV of inter-rupture intervals should match Ω/2 across systems.
  This is M1's prediction but applied phenomenologically — the CV
  of subjective-now-event reports should match CRR's CV.

These are non-trivial empirical predictions that are not stated
in canonical philosophy-of-time literature.

## Tier assessment

**T1 (philosophical pathway).**

Justification: CRR's δ(now) is structurally privileged in the
formalism, but the philosophical claim that this constitutes an
*ontological* present (rather than a *structural* feature of a
particular dynamical system) is a metaphysical commitment that
goes beyond the formalism. To reach T2-equivalent, CRR would need
to show that *any* successful CRR system *requires* an ontological
present in the philosophical sense — which it does not.

**Possible tier upgrade pathway:** if the predicted CV match
between subjective-now-event reports and CRR Ω/2 were empirically
established, this would push Ph3 to T2-equivalent (a quantitative
phenomenological regularity tied to the formal δ(now)).

T3-equivalent and T4-equivalent **not** awarded: predictions are
testable in principle but not yet established; no independent
engagement.

## Honest disclosure

CRR's δ(now) framing **does commit to a non-eternalist
metaphysics** — not merely a perspectival "now", but a
structurally-special instant. Users of CRR should be aware that
adopting the framework includes signing on to (or at least
sympathising with) presentism / growing-block over eternalism.
This is a strong metaphysical position; the campaign records it
as such.
