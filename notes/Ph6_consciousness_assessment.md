# Ph6 — Assessment: Conscious experience at coherence-rupture interface

## The philosophical claim

Conscious experience arises at the *interface* between coherence
accumulation (C-process) and rupture threshold (δ-event). Not in
the accumulation alone, not at the rupture alone, but at the
*meeting*.

This is a strong metaphysical claim about the location of
phenomenal consciousness in the dynamics of any system that
implements the CRR cycle. It connects to:

- **Integrated Information Theory (IIT)** (Tononi 2004; Tononi &
  Koch 2015): consciousness corresponds to integrated information
  Φ; complex of maximum Φ at any moment is the conscious system.
- **Global Workspace Theory** (Baars 1988; Dehaene & Naccache
  2001): consciousness is broadcast / ignition in a workspace.
- **Predictive processing / active inference** (Friston, Clark,
  Hohwy 2013): consciousness as model-update under prediction-
  error minimisation.
- **Whitehead's panexperientialism** (each actual occasion has
  some "subjective immediacy"): structurally compatible.
- **Edge-of-chaos / criticality theories of consciousness**
  (Werner 2007; Toker et al. 2022): consciousness peaks at
  critical-state dynamics.

## CRR-side formal mapping

- **C-process** ↔ unconscious accumulation (preconscious
  integration of evidence).
- **δ(now)** ↔ "moment of becoming conscious" / global-workspace
  ignition / IIT-Φ-max moment.
- **Coherence-rupture interface (region near C·Ω = 1)** ↔ the
  *experience itself*.
- **Beauty-peak at C* − Ω (Ph4)** ↔ peak-experience location.

## Metaphorical / Structural / Exact assessment

**Currently metaphorical, with structural ambitions and
quantitative content waiting on operationalisation.**

What is *metaphorical*:
- The very identification "conscious experience IS the
  coherence-rupture interface" is a *labelling* of formal
  structure with a phenomenological category. The formal
  structure (a particular dynamical regime) does not, by itself,
  imply consciousness.
- Whether the C-rupture interface is conscious in any
  Chalmers-hard-problem sense is a question CRR cannot answer
  any more than IIT, GWT, or active inference can.

What is *structural*:
- CRR shares structural commitments with IIT, GWT, predictive
  processing, criticality theories. These commitments include:
  past-state integration (C-process), discrete experiential
  events (δ-events), edge-of-chaos peak (C* − Ω). CRR is a
  *legible* contribution to the consciousness-science theoretical
  pluralism, not a category-error claim.

What could be *exact* (with operationalisation):
- IF "phenomenal experience" is operationalised as "what the
  system reports / what causes downstream effects identifiable
  as access-consciousness markers", THEN CRR's predictions
  about correlate timing (M22 + Ph4 → events at C* − Ω) become
  empirically specifiable.

## Phenomenological predictions

Does CRR predict a regularity not in the consciousness-science
literature?

1. **Conscious-event timing should peak at C* − Ω, not at C·Ω = 1.**
   M/EEG studies of perceptual decision-making and conscious
   access (Sergent et al. 2005; Dehaene's "global ignition"
   framework) report a ~300 ms latency from stimulus to
   conscious access. CRR predicts this latency corresponds to
   the C* − Ω location in coherence-of-evidence space — not to
   the rupture itself. Testable.

2. **CV of conscious-event intervals should match Ω/2** for
   a given system's substrate. For SO(2)-substrate systems
   (the "default" identification), this gives CV ≈ 1/(4π) ≈
   0.080. The CV of inter-event intervals between subjective
   "noticings" in mind-wandering paradigms (Schooler et al.
   2011+) is partially testable.

3. **Anti-correlation ρ = −1/2** (M11) between perception and
   action conscious channels. Testable in BCI (B4) and
   psychophysics.

4. **Class-A/B/C ordering of subjective time-perception**
   (B2, B5 connection): regulated cohorts (athletes,
   meditators) should subjectively experience time differently
   from normal-cohort and from noise-dominated (clinical)
   cohorts.

These predictions are **CRR-specific, not generic
consciousness-science predictions**.

## Independent engagement

No peer-reviewed engagement of CRR's coherence-rupture-interface
account with the consciousness-science literature. Adjacent work
(Werner 2007 *Brain dynamics across levels of organization*;
Toker et al. 2022 *Consciousness is supported by near-critical
slow cortical electrodynamics*) does not reference CRR.

The closest theoretical neighbour appears to be Friston's active
inference + Markov-blanket framing (CRR-FEP correspondence,
CAMPAIGN.md PART I) — but the specific identification of
consciousness-with-interface is not in canonical FEP literature.

## Tier assessment

**T1 (philosophical pathway).**

Justification: the CRR consciousness-at-interface claim has
explicit structural mapping to neighbouring theories but does not
yet have a successful empirical reconstruction. The metaphorical
component (labelling formal structure as phenomenal experience)
is unavoidable for any consciousness theory; CRR's specific
contribution is **the C* − Ω timing prediction**, which is
empirically tractable.

**Tier upgrade pathway:**
- **T2-equivalent** if the CV ≈ Ω/2 prediction for conscious-
  event intervals is empirically established (B5 EEG cohort
  work would contribute).
- **T3-equivalent** if the C*−Ω timing prediction is
  pre-registered and confirmed against M/EEG conscious-access
  latency data (Dehaene-style paradigms).

T4-equivalent **not** awarded: no independent engagement.

## Honesty disclosure

Like any consciousness theory, CRR cannot answer the *hard
problem* of why any formal structure should give rise to
phenomenal experience. It contributes to the *easy problem*
side (operational identification of conscious correlates) and
**does not claim more than this**. Marketing CRR as "explaining
consciousness" without this caveat would violate the campaign's
honesty discipline.

## Applied usefulness for 2026 and beyond

- **Anaesthesia depth monitoring:** current monitors (BIS,
  SedLine, CONOX, Narcotrend) use proprietary EEG-derived
  indices. CRR's parameter-free CV ≈ Ω/2 gauge for conscious-
  event intervals is a candidate open-source supplement,
  particularly for transitions through awareness (induction,
  emergence, intra-operative awareness detection).
- **Disorders-of-consciousness assessment:** vegetative-state /
  minimally-conscious-state / locked-in differential diagnosis
  (Coma Recovery Scale-Revised augmentation). CRR-derived
  rupture-event statistics could supplement clinical assessment
  in unresponsive patients (NIH 2026+ funding for DoC
  biomarkers).
- **AI-consciousness-evaluation suites:** as frontier models
  (Claude Opus 4.7+, GPT-5+, Gemini 3+) approach AGI, evaluation
  of model-internal "consciousness-like" properties becomes
  research-relevant. CRR provides parameter-free structural
  markers (CV of token-emission rupture events, coherence-
  trajectory analysis) that are *not* species-specific.
- **Mind-wandering / attention regulation:** consumer-EEG
  wearables (Muse, Neurable, Emotiv) increasingly track
  attention; CRR-derived class-label of attention state
  (Class A / B / C) is ready for integration.
- **Meditation / contemplative-neuroscience:** decades of
  research on meditation-induced altered states (Lutz et al.
  2008+; recent psychedelic-research revival) lacks
  parameter-free phenomenological metrics. CRR's framework
  gives measurable correlates (CV shifts, rupture-event statistics
  during meditation onset / sustained / emergence).

Ph6's applied potential is significant in clinical /
neurotechnology domains where consciousness-related metrics are
operationally consumed.
