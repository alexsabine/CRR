# Prior art for P14-EMPTY1 (read before freezing; 2026-10-07)

**The construction is standard.** Propagating a Kalman filter through a step with no observation (prediction only; P⁻ = A P Aᵀ + Q with no measurement update) is the textbook treatment of missing data: Anderson & Moore, *Optimal Filtering* (1979), §3.4 and §5; Bar-Shalom, Li & Kirubarajan, *Estimation with Applications to Tracking and Navigation* (2001), §5.2 (missing and delayed measurements) and §5.5 (out-of-sequence measurements); Harvey, *Forecasting, Structural Time Series Models and the Kalman Filter* (1989), §3.4.7 (missing observations); Shumway & Stoffer, *Time Series Analysis and Its Applications* (4th ed., 2017), §6.4 (missing data). Innovation gating (the χ² validation gate) is in Bar-Shalom et al. §3 and in every tracking text. Stale or duplicated sensor messages are a known failure mode in data association and sensor-fusion engineering (e.g. "stale track" and "duplicate detection" handling in Bar-Shalom et al. Ch. 11; Blackman & Popoli, *Design and Analysis of Modern Tracking Systems*, 1999, Ch. 6).

**What is therefore not new.** EMPTY, the candidate, is the missing-observation filter applied after a bit-identical repeat is recognised as a dropped update. The P14 enquiry says so in advance ("Null evidence is an identity on the likelihood update, not identity on the world transition … a native-filter tie is a useful reduction"). This study does not claim novelty for the construction (`CONSTITUTION/EPISTEMIC_TYPES.md`: novelty N0, specificity S1).

**What the study tests.** An engineering claim about a specific failure mode: when a channel re-emits its last value without a flag, treating the repeat as empty evidence and propagating the transition beats (i) treating it as a fresh observation (HOLD), (ii) the standard innovation gate (GATED), and (iii) recognising the repeat but freezing the state (FROZEN, the CRR ablation that removes transition propagation). The two negative controls are the cases where the claim must fail: repeats that are genuine evidence, and a world that does not move.

**Why it is in the CRR programme.** The "empty cut" commitment (A3: the cut has no content) is read here only as the engineering rule "do not invent an observation where there is none". The study cannot bear on A3 as metaphysics (`EPISTEMIC/GUARDRAILS.md` P-G4) and does not try to; a pass is an R4 declared-synthetic engineering result at most, a tie is a reduction to the native filter.

**Searches run.** None beyond the texts above were needed: the construction is textbook. No search for the exact stale-channel comparison was attempted; the claim is not novelty.
