# Admissibility report (written before the held-out draw)

* **Learnability and genuine new learning (C2).** Three screens per episode (`PREREG.md`). On development, 120 of 120 episodes passed all three. The frozen learner's mean J is about 0.89 against about 0.44 for SEC v2, so a frozen model cannot pass the J criterion. The H1 guards additionally require that the selected design learns task B better than SEC v2 and retains task A within 0.05 nats of it.
* **Bridge.** No CRR ontology is claimed. The only CRR-derived quantities are the Fisher–Rao path quantities in H3. They are computed on the model's task-A output distributions over observed probes, with the core's definitions (`CORE/MATHEMATICS.md` §I and §III), and their status is a test of usefulness, not of CRR.
* **Leakage.** Every feature is computed from quantities a learner has at step 100 of task B: the anchor, the stored end-of-A quantities, the checkpoints of its own trajectory, the observed probes and task-B data. The territory test sets are used only to record outcomes. The H2/H3 predictors are fitted on development episodes only.
* **Surrogates.** The by-construction blind condition (`hideA`) and the label-corrupting condition (`concept`) act as must-fail surrogates for any monitor that trusts its observations. The informative conditions (`full`, `alias`, `drift`, `hideA+ref`) act as must-not-abstain controls for H4.
* **Seen data.** All development episodes (seeds 10000–10119 with draw seed 101) and the 40 ablation histories (the first 40 of the same draws) are SEEN. The held-out seeds start at 200000 or above.
