# SEC-PG-LLM-H: prior art (written 2026-10-08, before the freeze)

**Method.** A targeted search for SEC-PG's specific combination, run as a session workflow. Four search angles were used:

* delayed or two-phase projection;
* projection combined with a Fisher or EWC anchor;
* projection in Transformer language models;
* projection of the optimiser update, and projection failing as models mature or widen.

Each of the 32 cited sources was opened by a second agent in a separate context, which checked the title, authors and quoted sentence; all 32 were confirmed. The operator re-opened two items (Octopus, arXiv 2605.14938v1; Spurious Forgetting, arXiv 2501.13453v1) and confirmed their titles, authors, versions and abstract statements. Raw record: `studies/SEC-PG-LLM-2026-10-08/research/prior_art_search_raw.json`. The table below keeps the items most relevant to the schedule. Separate-context agents are not independent reviewers.

**Bounding record.** The Phoenix, Ashes and Embers SEC record and the SEC-OBS1 reviews (`studies/SEC-OBS1-2026-10-08/SEC_OBS1_PRIOR_ART.md`) cover EWC, online EWC, GPM and their 2026 relatives; that record is not repeated here.

## Prior art

We searched for five things: (a) delayed projection, (b) projection combined with a Fisher anchor, (c) projection in Transformer LMs, (d) projection of the optimizer update, and (e) soft projection, or projection that hurts as models mature or widen. We opened every item below, and each quote is verbatim.

|Item|What it does|Overlap with SEC-PG|Quoted sentence; version|
|---|---|---|---|
|Octopus (Liu et al.)|LoRA on a 7B multimodal LLM: an unconstrained stage, then a soft orthogonality penalty. This beats keeping the constraint on throughout (Last 71.01 vs 61.18).|Closest to (a). Soft penalty, not GPM projection; no Fisher anchor.|"To address this challenge, we draw inspiration from annealing schedule [ 16 ] and propose a two-stage finetuning strategy, which enables model to first explore optimal solution in current data without constrains, and subsequently perform constrained refinement around local optimal." arXiv 2605.14938v1 (2026).|
|BECAME (Li et al.)|Trains with GPM-family projection, continues unconstrained, then merges the two using a Fisher-derived coefficient. |Spurious Forgetting (Zheng et al.)|Synthetic LM: old-task accuracy falls from about 100% to 10% within 150 new-task steps. **What is not anticipated, as far as this search found.** No opened item switches on hard GPM projection of the AdamW update partway through a new task while keeping a light diagonal-Fisher anchor on throughout, and none tests this on small byte-level Transformer LMs. Every component is published: activation bases (GPM), update projection after Adam (Adam-NSCL, Adam-GP, AlphaEdit), Fisher plus projection (NCL), LLM projection (OSFT), and a plastic-then-constrained order (Octopus). The claim under test is an engineering combination, novelty N0, because no item shows the exact schedule. Spurious Forgetting argues against the delay: it locates forgetting in exactly the early steps that SEC-PG leaves unprojected.

**What this search did not cover.** A possibly close "Dual-Stage Gradient Projection" paper (Wen et al., OpenReview o4VooeFajh) could not be opened. Theses, code repositories and venues without arXiv versions were not searched systematically. We found no reviewed source reporting that projection becomes net harmful with width or longer pretraining. That does not show none exists. Several items are unreviewed 2026 preprints.
