# Levin, Watson and CRR

Checked 2026-10-05. This file distinguishes published/source claims from Phoenix interpretation.

## Michael Levin — current programme

Levin's Tufts research programme studies morphological and behavioural information processing, collective intelligence of cells, bioelectric control, synthetic organisms and diverse intelligence.

Useful 2026 sources:
- Levin & Watson, **“Machines all the way up and cognition all the way down”**, *Seminars in Cell & Developmental Biology* 177–178 (2026), 103668. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41734513/
- Levin & Resnik, **“Mind Everywhere… Part Two”**, *Biological Theory* (2026). https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13752-025-00524-5
- Levin lab 2026 publications: https://as.tufts.edu/biology/levin-lab/publications
- Levin, **“Ingressing Minds”** current 2026 preprint/publication trajectory: https://sciety.org/articles/activity/10.31234/osf.io/5g2xj_v2
- Levin's 2026 short Platonic-space argument: https://thoughtforms.life/a-short-argument-on-platonic-space-variable-agency-patterns-that-in-form-physics-biology-computer-science-and-cognitive-science/

The peer-reviewed 2026 Levin/Watson paper argues against a simple threshold separating mechanism from cognition and promotes multiscale cognitive competencies. Levin/Resnik's TAME programme explicitly prizes empirically testable mentalistic concepts as interaction protocols rather than merely verbal attributions.

## Richard Watson — relevant programme

Relevant sources:
- Watson, **“Agency, Goal-Directed Behavior, and Part-Whole Relationships in Biological Systems”**, *Biological Theory* 19 (2024): 22–36. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13752-023-00447-z
- Buckley, Lewens, Levin, Millidge, Tschantz & Watson, **“Natural Induction: Spontaneous Adaptive Organisation without Natural Selection”**, *Entropy* 26 (2024), 765. https://www.mdpi.com/1099-4300/26/9/765
- Watson & Levin, **“The collective intelligence of evolution and development”** (2023).
- Southampton profile/publications: https://www.southampton.ac.uk/people/5x27lw/professor-richard-watson

Natural Induction is especially relevant: coupled physical relaxation/optimization and slower structural learning can generate adaptive organisation without system-level supervised reward or natural selection. Watson's agency work emphasises organised part–whole relations whose goal-directed behaviour exceeds that of isolated parts.

## Similarities with CRR

Potential structural correspondences:
- cognition/agency need not begin at a privileged scale;
- history can change the future competence of a system;
- organisation is relational and multiscale;
- learning and morphology can be treated as processes rather than static representations;
- higher-level wholes may acquire capabilities not usefully described by their isolated parts.

These are correspondences. They do not establish CRR.

## Differences

### Levin's Platonic-space proposal
Levin explicitly entertains a structured **nonphysical** space of patterns that can ingress into physical embodiments; some patterns may have agency.

### Phoenix historical-immanent reading
The user's CRR proposal is almost the reverse:
> forms are the persistent products of realised finite history — the "choices" made at occasions — rather than already-complete entities in a separate realm.

This can be written schematically:

```text
Levin:
structured latent / Platonic patterns
        ↓ ingress
physical embodiment

Phoenix-CRR hypothesis:
finite distinctions + realised occasions
        ↓ historical constraint / regeneration
persistent form
```

This is a **real metaphysical disagreement**, not a terminological difference.

### Watson / Natural Induction
Watson's work is more naturally compatible with an immanent historical account because adaptive organisation can arise from recurrent physical optimization and learning. But CRR still has to show whether its arc/cut/surplus vocabulary predicts anything beyond Watson's established mechanics.

## Research opportunity

Ask whether synthetic morphologies that surprise genotype/environment models are better predicted by:
1. a structured latent-space prior;
2. physical/history-conditioned adaptive organisation;
3. CRR-specific historical quantities;
4. hybrid models.

The experiment must be designed with Levin/Watson expertise before claiming to discriminate metaphysics.
