## 4. CRR among falsifiable philosophies and heuristics: a fair comparison

**Where CRR stands on these dimensions.** It has fixed no free quantity (the one attempt failed); it has frozen and tested risky predictions, more than most metaphysical programmes ever do, and every one of them was a bridge; it has recorded every failure and responded by weakening, which is the degenerating pattern Lakatos names, honestly labelled by the programme itself; it has corroborated no novel fact. Its *method* belongs with steady-state cosmology, IIT's adversarial test and Smolin's bound; its *content* belongs with Whitehead, Bergson and the Growing Block; its *logical status* is the FEP's. The Ashes conclusion stands and is the fairest single line in the record: "CRR is a correct abstraction of what many sciences independently had to discover; it has not yet told any of them something they did not know."
