## 1. Platonic space: Levin's claim, exactly, and CRR's interpretation

### 1.1 What Levin claims (the 2026 text)

- **The space.** Never formally defined. Its operative characterisation (p. 21): it "can be systematically studied", "is structured, and thus can be traversed by thought and experiment to find new 'adjacent' patterns", "matters for what happens in the physical world (via ingressions of these patterns)", and "meets the engineer's definition of what is ontologically real: whatever the engineer has to worry about or exploit". Ontology is explicitly declined: "I eschew questions about whether the space is 'ontologically real'" (Overview (e), pp. 3–4).
- **The hypotheses** (Overview (g), p. 4): patterns in-forming form and function "inhabit a space very different from our physical world"; they span "a very wide spectrum of agency" from the digits of Feigenbaum's constant to "behavioral policies … kinds of minds"; they are "functionally causal and interact with physical systems (likely, bi-directionally) … an explicit interactionist model but it does not require quantum effects"; "Bodies … are interfaces for a massive, multi-scale hierarchy of patterns to ingress into the physical world … we are patterns."
- **Ingression and the pointer** (pp. 9, 28): used without definition; the gloss is that complexity is "not so much contained or compressed in the seed … but rather pointed to by the seed and retrieved by a process that spans both the world of patterns and the world of physical pointers"; "you get more out than you put in"; fn 16: "basically a variant of the receiver theory of the mind-brain relationship".
- **Formal content.** None. No equations, metric, model or measure. The nearest is the "delta" principle (pp. 32, 43): "quantify the effort put in and the competencies that emerge; the delta between them directly sheds light on what and how much can be obtained from the Platonic Space". Neither "effort" nor "competency" is operationalised.
- **Does the library learn?** Posed, not claimed. "Platonic forms are eternal and unchanging" is rejected: "some might be, while others can change over 'time'" (p. 4); fn 1 (new in 2026): "I don't yet have a full account of how time might work at the interface of Platonic space and physical spacetime"; §4 moves toward "a phylogeny of Platonic forms" and patterns "that can even learn from experience" (pp. 22–24); the conclusion asks (p. 45): "can its projection into the physical world feed back to modify the patterns and ways in which they will ingress in the future? … perhaps acts of engineering or biological procreation somehow pinch off and mold a region of that space which will be modified by its experiences."
- **Falsifiability** (both passages new in 2026; the 2025 text has none): "falsifiable in the same way all significant scientific ideas are falsifiable: if concerted effort … reveals no quantifiable input from the Platonic Space that isn't captured by conventional accounting frameworks, and if these ideas result [in] no new discoveries or capabilities at the bench, then it can be abandoned" (p. 4); "unlikely to be refutable or established by a single experiment … No single observation could establish such a thing because physicalism and emergence are plastic enough that almost anything … can be accommodated by them as epicycles … If, after some number of years of concerted effort this model has not driven any advances … it can be shelved … The model makes specific predictions that break with current computational and other frameworks and time will tell" (pp. 44–45). No such prediction is stated anywhere in the paper. The evaluation criterion is "fecundity in driving novel, interesting research programs" (p. 5).
- **Caveats in his own words:** "I prefer, but cannot prove, the working hypothesis" (p. 9); "I can't disprove this [emergentism]" (p. 21); patterns "must be observed/detected/inferred by an observer … a diadic relation between living systems observing each other" (fn 6); "it takes minds to recognize minds" (fn 17); "The only thing that can be said strongly at this point is that our ignorance … is vast" (p. 45).

## 2. The Fields–Glazebrook–Levin framework, mathematics beside mathematics

### 2.1 Their objects, as stated

Textbook finite-dimensional quantum theory; a bit as the quantum of information; `ħ` the action to obtain one bit; Landauer `ln 2 k_B T` per bit (2022, §2.1). `U = AB`; separability `S(|AB⟩) = 0`; in the weak-interaction limit `H_AB = β^k k_B T^k Σ_i α_i^k M_i^k` (Eq. 6) with mutually commuting ±1 observables: "Physical interaction = (thermodynamics) · (yes/no questions)". The boundary `B` is "an ancillary construct, not 'part of' either A or B", realised as qubits alternately prepared and measured; "A's actions are B's sensations and vice-versa" (Fig. 1). Quantum reference frames (QRFs) are physical systems encoding unmeasurable phase information; a choice of QRF is a choice of computational basis; whether a system implements a QRF is undecidable (Rice). Agency: "A (nontrivial) agent is a system A with an internal dynamics `H_A` that breaks the `S_N` swap symmetry of its boundary" (Definition 1, §2.5); the broken symmetry sorts the boundary into sectors `E` (observed environment), `F` (free-energy source and sink), `Y` (memory). Time: "A can only use changes in `ρ_E` if it can write time-ordered records … to, and subsequently read them from, the memory sector Y. Here `t_A` is an A-specific time coordinate that must be constructed" (§3.1); any memory with more than one record defines a clock `G_ij` which "must, in general, be a groupoid operator"; "Internal time `t_S` … is discrete … coarse-grained with respect to the background time `t`"; non-commuting QRFs make `G_ij ∘ G_ji ≠ G_ji ∘ G_ij`, so "noncommutativity forces `t_A` to be unidirectional, and hence memory records to be encoded irreversibly with an accompanying expenditure of free energy. The internal clock `G_ij` thus defines `t_A` as an entropic time" (§3.4). Learning: an operator `L: (M^A_E(i), [ρ_E(i)]) ↦ M^A_E(i+1)` (Eq. 28); prediction error `Er_E(k) = d(M^A_E(k), M_E(k))` (Eq. 30), a metric on Markov kernels. The quantum FEP: "A generic quantum system A will act so as to minimize `Er_X` for each deployable QRF X" (p. 31); asymptotically "The FEP is, asymptotically, the Principle of Unitarity" (§4.3). Identity: an identified system is `S = PR`, pointer and reference; "identifiability of S requires `[ρ_R(i)] = [ρ_R(j)]` for all i, j" (§3.3). Quantum against classical memory: "Classical memories are at risk of environmental perturbation, unless sufficient free energy is devoted to maintaining them. Quantum 'memories' are encoded by the dynamics `P_A` and are at risk … only if separability breaks down" (pp. 23–24). *Thoughts and thinkers* adds: "time is built into the concept of interaction: it is what separates input from output … strictly system-relative" (p. 8); "Processes in E … can be observed and manipulated only against a background of stasis. Time enables stasis" (pp. 8–9); persistence "is thus a combination of observed distinctions … and a hypothetical process … what happens between observations" (p. 12); "The lineage of information is, therefore, not tree-structured, but rather a backward- and forward-branching structure in which the past of any item of information may be as ambiguous as its future" (p. 23). Predictions (2022 §5.4, verbatim): "1. The internal, molecular-scale dynamics of both prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells implement quantum information processing … 2. Interacting biological systems … can be expected to display quantum contextuality and violations of the Bell and Leggett-Garg inequalities." Their status: "These predictions remain largely untested … Neither Bell nor Leggett-Garg inequality violations have been conclusively demonstrated with biological systems."

## 3. REBUS, ALBUS and canalization, with the executable check

### 3.1 Their formal content, exactly

Carhart-Harris & Friston 2019 contains no equations. Its structure (Fig. 1; §II.B): hierarchical predictive coding; two precisions, "the precision (ascribed importance or salience) afforded to the ascending prediction errors (surprise) and the precision (felt confidence) of posterior beliefs"; "Precision is formally inverse variance … encoded by the sensitivity or postsynaptic gain of neuronal populations" (fn, p. 319). Relaxation is a gain change on deep-pyramidal (prior-encoding) populations, preferentially at the top (DMN, 5-HT2A density). The landscape (fn 5, p. 319): "a loss of precision (confidence) in posterior beliefs corresponds to a decrease in the curvature or flattening of the free-energy landscape … Flat minima lend neuronal activity a latitude for fluctuations that characteristically result in critical slowing." Annealing (p. 319): "a system is heated … attains a state of heightened plasticity … as the drug is metabolized and the system cools, its dynamics begin to stabilize and attractor basins begin to steepen again. This process may result in the emergence of a new energy landscape with revised properties." Two phases: "1) a lightening or relaxation of the precision weighting on priors and 2) allows for a (potentially enduring) revision of such priors". The entropy link is asserted, not derived: "increased brain entropy reflects a relaxation of the precision weighting of priors" (§II.I), with the authors' own distinction that "The entropic brain measures the uncertainty of neuronal fluctuations across time, whereas free-energy measures the uncertainty of beliefs encoded by neuronal fluctuations." Post-acute mechanism: Bayesian model reduction, "fact-free learning"; "a flattening of the relevant free-energy (attractor) landscape postacutely … an attenuated vestige"; "the brain is more sensitive to simple sensory prediction error post-treatment" (§II.F.3).

ALBUS (Safron 2024): also no equations; the direction of the precision change depends on dose and level ("Low-to-moderate levels of stimulation of L5 excitatory pyramidal neurons may be more likely to strengthen (SEBUS) … rather than weaken (REBUS)"); strengthening "primarily at intermediate levels of abstraction"; a second precision term REBUS omits (L2/3 interneurons suppressing ascending errors, "presently not included in REBUS models"); oscillatory encoding (priors as beta complexes, errors as gamma); one discriminating test proposed (illusion susceptibility: REBUS raises thresholds, SEBUS lowers them). SEBUS is a construct inside ALBUS, not a separate paper.

Canalization (2023): "Canalization encodes a resistance to change via model precision"; "variance can be called 'inverse precision' … an increase in phenotypic volatility or plasticity manifests as an increase in learning-rate"; "precision … corresponds to the curvature of the free-energy landscape … canalization is like a river, carving its own riverbed, where the depth of the eroded valley corresponds to precision"; TEMP ("Temperature or Entropy Mediated Plasticity") "subsumes increased learning rate, (Ising) temperature and entropy"; and the two-phase carryover: "increased temperature will have an acute and later phase carryover effect, equivalent to rebalancing the landscape by reducing its average skew or curvature, i.e., its precision … would alter not just the energy or exploration rate within a state space, but also the shape of the space itself, after the system has cooled" (fetched text, lines 293–297). Deane 2020 gives the learning-rate reading: "relaxing of high-level priors … means the system adopts a very high Bayesian learning rate", hence "a collapse in the temporal thickness of deep generative models, and a collapse in the temporal depth of the corresponding self-model" (§§4.4, 5).
