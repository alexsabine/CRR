# Platonic space: a controlled disagreement

## The question

Where do persistent forms come from?

## Levin-style proposal (source interpretation)
Levin's recent Platonic-space programme proposes a structured space of mathematical/morphological/cognitive patterns; physical systems can function as pointers/interfaces through which patterns ingress. He explicitly presents this as speculative but experimentally actionable.

## Phoenix-CRR proposal (user hypothesis)
Persistent forms are **productions of realised time**:
- finite systems make distinctions;
- occasions settle;
- settled constraints alter what can continue;
- repeated closure makes stable forms.

The phrase "choices at every moment" is metaphysical shorthand and must not be treated as literal human-like choice in all systems.

## Why this matters

The two views can agree on the observed form while disagreeing about ontology.

CRR cannot win by redescribing Levin's examples historically after seeing them. It would need a frozen discriminating consequence.

## Candidate discrimination programme

Choose synthetic systems with:
- controlled initial embodiment/genome;
- controlled environmental history;
- repeated perturbation;
- independently measured adaptive trajectories.

Compare:
- history-free structural prior;
- history-conditioned standard dynamical model;
- Watson-style adaptive organisation model;
- CRR-restricted historical model.

A CRR result must specify **what it predicts that a generic history-dependent model does not**.

## Epistemic status

`ONTOLOGY / OPEN`.

Neither Platonic ingression nor historical-immanent form is established by the current CRR mathematics.
