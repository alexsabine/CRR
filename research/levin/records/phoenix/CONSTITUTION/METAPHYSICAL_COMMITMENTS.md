# Provisional metaphysical commitments

These are **ONTOLOGY**, not empirical results.

## M1 — realised history / unrealised possibility
Realised events and unrealised possibilities are ontologically distinct in the proposed Growing-Block reading.

## M2 — the present as boundary
The present is treated as an empty boundary of settlement rather than a thin duration containing independent content.

## M3 — local becoming
Becoming is local to finite systems. A global universal "Now" is not supplied by the current rotor mathematics.

## M4 — historical constraint
Successor organisation can depend on what has actually settled. The mathematics can enforce non-anticipation; this alone does not prove ontological openness.

## M5 — maps are not histories
A prediction, simulation or latent representation of a future is not the realised event itself.

## M6 — finitude enables relation
A finite boundary makes distinguishable relation possible. This is a philosophical commitment/interpretation, not a theorem of Fisher geometry.

## M7 — historical-immanent forms (Phoenix hypothesis)
A CRR-friendly alternative to separate Platonic space is that persistent forms are **productions of realised history**: stable relational constraints made by finite events/choices and inherited forward.

This is the user's metaphysical proposal. It is not derived by `CORE/MATHEMATICS.md`, and it must not be presented as if it were.

Epigraphic framing:
> “Eternity is in love with the productions of time.” — William Blake

Phoenix reading:
> Eternity is approached through the finite productions that persist, not assumed as a separate inventory of already-complete forms.
