# Register of NOT DECIDABLE comparisons (opened 2026-10-07)

**Rule (from `FOUNDATIONS/FALSIFIABLE_METAPHYSICS_DIRECTION.md`).** If two readings induce the same probability law for all permitted interventions, they are empirically equivalent in that experiment; the ontological comparison is recorded NOT DECIDABLE and mathematical or heuristic work continues. This register makes the record explicit so that an undecidable comparison is neither quietly promoted nor quietly dropped. One row per comparison; rows are appended and dated, never rewritten.

Each row carries: the two readings; the equivalence argument (what makes every permitted intervention agree); the **structure both readings share**, which is what Phoenix may claim; an intervention that would break the equivalence, if anyone has one; the commitment Phoenix holds and the non-empirical grounds for holding it; and the review date.

**How to use a row.** (1) Cite the shared structure, never the reading, in any claim outside this file. (2) When a breaking intervention appears, open an enquiry and move the row to "decidable, pending" with the ID. (3) At each quarterly review count the rows: a growing register with no row moving to decidable is the pressure test's signal to reduce the programme's scientific scope, not to erase its philosophical value (`FOUNDATIONS/FALSIFIABLE_METAPHYSICS_DIRECTION.md`). (4) A row is never closed by an engineering pass or failure (P-G4).
