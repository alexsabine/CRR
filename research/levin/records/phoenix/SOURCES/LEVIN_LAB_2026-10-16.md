# Levin Lab: current research enquiries, data-sets and code (survey of 2026-10-16, OD-51)

**Scope.** Every 2026 output of Michael Levin's lab (Allen Discovery Center at Tufts) and lab members, as of
2026-10-16:
- 18 peer-reviewed papers dated 2026, plus 17 Allen Discovery Center–Tufts papers without Levin;
- 33 Levin-coauthored preprints first posted in 2026;
- every public code repository and data-set found.

**Method.** Three parallel searches:
- PubMed and PMC full text (data-availability statements read in the JATS XML);
- the lab's preprint and topic pages, the arXiv API, the bioRxiv API by DOI, and Europe PMC preprint search;
- GitHub (`git ls-remote` and web pages), NCBI GEO/SRA through E-utilities, the Zenodo API, and the lab's software
  index (https://drmichaellevin.org/resources/software.html).

**Limits.**
- Mostly abstracts were read; full texts only where PMC had them.
- bioRxiv pages were rate-limited (HTTP 429) and Preprints.org refused requests (403), so preprint data statements
  are unread.
- OSF, Figshare and Dryad were not searched.
- Items marked *unverified* below were not confirmed.

Many unrelated authors named "M. Levin" were excluded by affiliation.

## 1. What the lab is asking now (themes, with the 2026 work in each)

1. **Memory and learning in non-neural living systems.**
   - Xenobots form distinct long-term memories of embryo extract and of ATP, visible in behaviour, transcripts and
     calcium (Pai et al., bioRxiv 2026.03.17.712168; data **GSE320387**).
   - Trained planaria keep a learned response through head regeneration (Dev, Nguyen, Levin, bioRxiv
     2026.08.10.741213).
   - A planarian's regeneration history, more than its social housing, shapes its transcriptional response to BaCl₂
     (Kapsetaki, Landsberger, Levin, bioRxiv 2026.07.28.741268; data **GSE341203**).
   - The "Cell Trainer" device for automated training of mammalian cells (Erickson et al., bioRxiv 2026.08.13.744473).
   - Bacteria form spatial patterns that encode the presence of embryos and Xenobots (Sergeeva et al., bioRxiv
     2026.09.02.748853).
   - Model systems:
     - learning in predator–prey ecosystems (Samanta, Hazan, Levin, arXiv 2605.30109);
     - problem solving by metabolic feedback (de Baat, Levin, bioRxiv 2026.05.07.721661);
     - associative conditioning in gene-regulatory networks (Pigozzi et al., Commun Biol; correction in 2026).
2. **Bioelectric control of form and physiology.**
   - Imaging over a whole life shows long-distance non-neural bioelectric patterns on timescales from seconds to
     hours (McMillen & Levin, Dev Biol 2026, 10.1016/j.ydbio.2026.08.001).
   - MicroRNAs regionalise bioelectric patterns (Egea-Carro et al., J Chem Phys 2026).
   - The inner nuclear membrane's voltage and chromatin texture depend on the history of ion exposure (Sediqi et al.,
     bioRxiv 2026.06.23.733978).
   - Compensating ion-channel mutations by modulating other channels (Hazan & Levin, bioRxiv 2026.09.20.753002; code
     and data released).
   - Electric-field feedback in prepatterning (Manicka & Levin, Cell Rep Phys Sci 2025; code).
3. **Cancer.**
   - Membrane voltage and connexin expression act together in tumour growth and metastasis (Grodstein, Mathews,
     Levin, bioRxiv 2026.09.02.748840; in silico).
   - Clofilium against colorectal cancer, with a new RNA-seq series (**GSE349165**, public 2026-10-15; paper link
     *unverified*).
   - How cancers and parasites take over host control, as staged control (Shmeleva & Levin, Semin Cell Dev Biol 2026).
   - Cancer as an analogy for AI externalities (Lyons, Pio-Lopez, Levin, Preprints 202604.0056).
4. **Ageing and longevity.**
   - Atavistic gene-expression dissociation in ageing (Pio-Lopez & Levin, Aging Cell 2026 issue; code).
   - *Theories of Aging: From Damage and Programmed Theories to Goal-Directedness* (Pio-Lopez, Jawanda, Levin,
     Preprints 202608.1219, v2 2026-09-22). It proposes ageing as a loss of goal-directedness as a third class of
     theory.
   - *Multi-Scale Longevity* (Pio-Lopez & Levin, Preprints 202602.1770).
   - Background: the ageing NCA (Adv Sci 2025), checked in Phoenix P23-AGING, -CRR and -COMPETENCY.
5. **Synthetic organisms, biobots and automation.**
   - Neurobots: Xenopus biobots with self-organised neural networks (Fotowat et al., Adv Sci 2026; **GSE295614**).
   - Purinergic signalling disrupts coordination in basal Xenobots (Varley, Pai, Levin, Bongard, bioRxiv
     2026.06.04.730190).
   - A closed-loop robot scientist, "MOMbot" (Bielawski et al., bioRxiv 2026.09.11.751076).
   - Learning language-conditioned interventions on Xenobots from archived data (Le, Blackiston, Levin, Bongard,
     arXiv 2610.02247).
6. **Computational models: NCAs, evolution and AI.**
   - BraiNCA (arXiv 2604.01932; ALIFE 2026).
   - Low-rank "regulatory handles" control shape in NCAs (Hartl et al., arXiv 2609.29755).
   - Diffusion-model evolution, HADES/CondEvo (Adv Sci 2026; code).
   - LoRA adapters on random scaffolds (arXiv 2604.08749).
   - Autotelic RL discovery in Lenia, CARL (arXiv 2608.26116), and Lenia agnosiophobia (arXiv 2605.30708).
   - Learnable novelty (Zhang & Levin, arXiv 2607.18433) and talking to gene-regulatory networks (arXiv 2605.16321).
   - Causal emergence before self-replication in an origin-of-life model (Pigozzi & Levin, arXiv 2607.28250) and in
     RL agents (arXiv 2605.06746).
   - Topological limits on self-organisation (Phil Trans A 2026).
7. **Diverse intelligence, philosophy and alignment.**
   - *Mind Everywhere* I and II (Biol Theory 2026).
   - *Machines all the way up* (Semin Cell Dev Biol 2026).
   - Cognitive glue as shared models of scarcity (Phil Trans A 2026).
   - Theories of consciousness beyond brains (Phil Trans A 2026).
   - The virtual governor, cognitive offloading, and cognition spaces.
   - LLMs as a cognitive virus, and Positive Alignment.
   - A Lancet Psychiatry Personal View on AI-associated delusions.
   - The fourth version of *Ingressing Minds*, the Platonic-space paper (PsyArXiv 5g2xj_v4, 2026-06-25).

**Balance.** In 2026 the work is mostly computational and theoretical: 15 arXiv and 6 Preprints.org items against
12 bioRxiv, of which about 8 contain wet-lab data. The experimental core is Xenopus and its constructs (Xenobots,
neurobots), planaria, human Anthrobots and cultured mammalian cells.

## 4. Where this meets Phoenix (P23 and M7)

The lab's 2026 experiments repeatedly ask the question Phoenix's commitment M7 asks: **does a living system carry
its history in a way that changes its future behaviour?**
- planarian memory surviving head regeneration;
- regeneration history shaping BaCl₂ responses;
- Xenobot memory of stimuli;
- the history of ion exposure shaping nuclear-membrane voltage.

Two of these have public raw data: **GSE341203** (planarian regeneration history) and **GSE320387** (Xenobot
memory). They allow, for the first time in Phoenix, a *real-data* version of the "same present, different past"
design used in P23-ALGOTYPES and P23-AGING: do organisms matched in present form, but differing in history, differ
in transcriptional state? And is that difference a stable hidden state, or does it decay?

Two caveats on the ageing work:
- The lab's 2026 *Theories of Aging* preprint states the "loss of goal-directedness" thesis tested in
  P23-AGING-COMPETENCY. That study found the thesis does not hold in the lab's own ageing model, where regenerative
  competence is constant with age and loss is a first passage past a critical lesion. That result concerns one
  computer model, not organisms.
- *Ingressing Minds* v4 continues the Platonic-space account that ND-2 records as NOT DECIDABLE against M7.

**Proposed next step.**
- Re-analyse GSE341203 under a frozen protocol, before any look at the data beyond its GEO metadata.
- Question: is the transcriptional effect of regeneration history (i) present in worms whose present phenotype is
  matched, (ii) larger than the effect of housing, as the authors report, and (iii) carried by a small number of
  persistent programs (a hidden state) rather than diffuse noise?

That would be an independent check of a 2026 finding with the lab's own public data.

## Correction (2026-10-16, about 16:05Z, OD-52)

The table in §2 and the proposal in §4 treat **GSE341203** as public. It is not. The GEO accession page, read on
2026-10-16 at about 16:05Z, says: "Accession "GSE341203" is currently private and is scheduled to be released on Jul
23, 2027." The preprint cites an accession that has not yet been released. The proposed planarian study is therefore
blocked until the release date. The programme's first study moves to **GSE320387**, the Xenobot memory series (Pai,
Traer, Sperry, Zeng & Levin, bioRxiv 10.64898/2026.03.17.712168), which is public. The "DOI link
*unverified*" for GSE320387 is now resolved: the bioRxiv API returns the title, authors and abstract for
**10.64898/2026.03.17.712168**, and they match the GEO series.
