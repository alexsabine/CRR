**Stage:** SEED. **Layer:** metaphysics/empirical bridge. **Evidence:** proposal only; no new registered empirical results.

Can historical constraint construction be distinguished from latent-form and Natural Induction accounts?

Compare latent priors, relaxation plus structural learning, historical memory and hybrids. Seek observable differences with expert input.

## Interpretation ceiling and falsifier

Record recovery/correspondence/lift/bridge/prediction separately. A learned latent space is not necessarily a separate Platonic ontology. No claim about latest papers is advanced without new retrieval.

The empirical claim fails if its frozen primary statistic misses its registered bound, if the strongest allowed rival wins, or if a required CRR ingredient can be removed without loss under the registered reduction margin. A control or admissibility failure closes this operationalisation; it cannot be repaired in place on exposed data. Formal counterexamples reject only the assumptions they address.

## Before graduation

Assign one primary hypothesis ID and exact parent-source rows. Fix measurable variables, units, causal bridge, rival degrees of freedom, independent unit, effect bound, aggregation and equality convention. Obtain power analysis for that bound; specify multiplicity, exclusions/NaNs, all seeds and sensitivity cells, data split/exposure, costs and stopping rule. Read primary prior art; run must-pass and must-fail controls. Only then create a claim-specific preregistration and external anchor. No numerical margin or sample size is silently inherited from a different programme.
