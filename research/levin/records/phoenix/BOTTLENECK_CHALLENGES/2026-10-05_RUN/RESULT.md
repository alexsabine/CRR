## Answer to the question asked

Processed against 43 bottlenecks named by their own authors in 2026, the frozen CRR core resolved none. Twenty-four were NO CONTACT, ten REPHRASE, eight REDUCES, zero CANDIDATE. In three cases the core's object, applied as the paper would need, gave the wrong answer, and each time for the same reason: on any noisy carrier the accumulated Fisher arc is dominated by noise path length. That defeats R6 as an entropy-production bound (C1: the "bound" exceeds the true value by 18× to 1581× as resolution improves), the R2 cut as a cycle-boundary detector (C3: thirty times looser than the polar protophase the paper already uses), and the arc trigger as an event-boundary rule (B3: 29 false alarms against 4 for the standard displacement rule). The core warns about this in §I and §IV; the challenges show the warning is decisive in practice.

Two things of value came out, neither a CRR result:

- **A transferable constraint** (B4): a 2026 planarian model built independently on "excess cost along the trajectory" plus "endpoint displacement" is rank-deficient if it uses both together with path cost. The core's identifiability note applies to it directly.
- **A live question** (B3): event-segmentation models may be missing a cumulative-change trigger alongside error and uncertainty triggers. The paper's data are public. The detector that would test it is the displacement-since-last-boundary rule, not the core's arc.

## What this says about CRR as falsifiable metaphysics

The intention was to test the metaphysics by running the equations against existing science. The equations ran. The result is that where the core makes contact it restates the field (temporal Fisher information, thermodynamic length, renewal ticks, memory resetting), and where it offers its own object (arc-based cut, arc-based trigger, R6 on trajectories) the object loses to the field's standard one on noisy data. No metaphysical commitment was reached by any of the 43 bottlenecks; the relational-time papers show that M3 as a stance does not supply the clock–world split they need. This is consistent with P28-FM1's finding that no metaphysical link has yet been placed at risk, and it adds a reason: the core's distinctive quantities do not survive contact with noise well enough to carry a bridge.

## What would change the verdict

A carrier on which the Fisher arc is not noise-dominated (a slowly evolving *distribution*, as in C5, rather than a single stochastic trajectory) is the only regime where `C` behaved as intended. Challenges in that regime, with a frozen rival and a differing prediction, are the ones worth registering. None of the 43 papers' bottlenecks lies there.
