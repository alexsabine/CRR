## Reading, check by check

**C1.** The clock of becoming is Kim's information length to nine digits on a Gaussian family, as the chain rule says it must be; the must-fail arm shows the identity is the Fisher metric's, not a coincidence of the example. The two-clocks check shows the one place CRR's clock and minimal physicalism's differ: MP counts classical writes (each at Landauer cost, each a cycle), CRR counts distinguishable change; 99 identical writes are 99 cycles and zero becoming. Neither is evidence for anything; it fixes which native object each is.

**C2.** The planarian record (SEEN) gives the one-`q` age kernel of CORE R5 an interval it cannot meet: per-round retention of the two-headed state is at least 0.990 at 95 % confidence, while head shape remodels within one cycle. The record's own reading ("two different kernels appear in the same organism, and no CRR rule says which applies where", `DATA_ACCESS.md` §2) now carries a number. The record's statement that accumulating precedence is "WRONG (stationary)" was stronger than the registered n supports: 8/30 against 0.23 has p = 0.38, and excluding a +0.04 drift needs about 740 animals per round. Minimal physicalism's P9 (stability by access frequency) reconciles both facts with a free parameter, which is to say it is compatible and untested; Levin's own bistable-attractor model reconciles them with no kernel at all. **What CRR would have to adopt to survive this record:** an access term in the kernel, which is MP's idea, not CRR's.

**C3.** The four worlds separate as follows. A fixed library (Levin's ingression with patterns that do not learn) is flat in precedence and unaffected by any physical act. A learning library local to the instance behaves exactly as a physical memory: it shows precedence, it does not transfer to a naive instance, and a physical reset erases it; F4's equivalence is confirmed with an intervention, and such a library is history-production in other words. A learning library *shared* across instances is the only version with an observable signature of its own: a naive instance draws as if it had the others' history (T2 = 0.001), and erasing one instance's physical record changes nothing (T3 = 0.004). **The honest statement for ND-2:** the ontological comparison "forms ingress from a Platonic space" against "forms are productions of realised history" becomes decidable only if the space is shared across physically disconnected instances or survives physical erasure; that prediction is morphic resonance in all but name **[memory: Sheldrake; tests unreplicated]**, and CRR's M7 denies it.
