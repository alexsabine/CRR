## C1 — The clock of becoming against Kim's information length and the minimal-physicalism cycle count

**Ingredient.** `C = (1/σ)∫√(θ̇ᵀ g θ̇) dt` (CORE I).
**Beside.** Kim's information rate `1/τ² = ∫ dx (1/p)(∂_t p)²` and length `L = ∫ dt/τ`; MP's clock as counted preparation–measurement cycles (writes).
**Check 1a (identity).** For `p(x | μ(t), s²)` Gaussian with `μ(t) = sin t`, `s = 0.7`: compute `1/τ²` by numerical differentiation and quadrature, and `θ̇ᵀ g θ̇ = μ̇²/s²`. **Prediction:** relative error `< 10⁻³` over the whole path; `L = σC` with `σ = 1`. Must-fail arm: the same comparison with `g` replaced by the Euclidean metric must not agree when `s ≠ 1`.
**Check 1b (two clocks).** A sequence of 200 "writes" of which the first 100 repeat one value and the last 100 move: MP's cycle count rises linearly throughout; `C` stays at 0 for the first 100 and rises after. **Prediction:** `C` after 100 cycles = 0 to machine precision; count = 100.
**Meaning.** 1a holds: `C` is REDUNDANT-IG (Kim's length). 1b holds: DISTINCTION between a clock that counts operations (MP, Landauer-costed) and one that counts distinguishable change (CRR); the two coincide only when every write is distinguishable from the last. Neither outcome is evidence for CRR.

## C2 — Three memory kernels against the planarian record (SEEN; R5; retrodictive)

**Ingredient.** The regeneration kernel over completed occasions: P3 age weights (fading, one `q`); P2/Pólya precedence (accumulating); beside MP's P9 (stability by access frequency) and Levin's bistable-attractor readout (hysteresis; `FORMS-AND-EASEMENT` F2).

**Check 2a.** Per-round retention `ρ` under a one-`q` fading kernel for the DH cohort: the exact one-sided 95 % lower bound on `ρ³` from 100/100 and 102/102, hence on `ρ`. **Prediction:** `ρ ≥ 0.985`. Combined with head-shape retention ≤ 0.5 per cycle, no single `q` fits both: the one-`q` age kernel is **WRONG** as a universal (the P06 finding again, now with an interval).
**Check 2b.** Pólya precedence on the cryptic cohort: starting weight 0.23, does 8/30 at round two reject stationarity? Exact binomial test; and the n per round needed to detect a drift of +0.04 and +0.10 at 80 % power. **Prediction:** not rejected at n = 30 (p > 0.3); n needed for +0.04 is > 500 per round. So `DATA_ACCESS.md` §2's "WRONG (stationary)" for precedence is **downgraded to NOT EXCLUDED at the registered n**; the supplement's larger-N claim is not quantified here.
**Check 2c.** Access-weighted stability (MP P9): a kernel `π_m ∝ q^{age} · (1 + a·accesses_m)` with the DH state re-written at every recut (accesses = rounds) and head shape never re-written. **Prediction:** one `(q, a)` pair reproduces both the DH persistence (≥ 0.985 per round) and head-shape decay (≤ 0.5) when `a` is large; the age-only kernel (`a = 0`) cannot. This is a check that MP's rule is *compatible*, not that it is right; the bistable attractor (Levin's own model) is compatible without any kernel.
**Meaning.** 2a WRONG for CRR's one-`q` reading; 2b corrects the record's own over-statement; 2c: REDUNDANT-DOMAIN for MP/Levin (the domain's model already says it) and a proposal that CRR's kernel, if it is to survive this record, needs an access term it does not have, credited to Fields' P9.

## C3 — The precedence signature and the only intervention that decides a Platonic library against history

**Ingredient.** M7: forms are productions of realised history. Rivals: a fixed library (Levin's ingression with fixed patterns), a *learning* library local to the instance (F4's equivalence), and a *shared* learning library across instances (ingression from a common space that learns from use anywhere).
**Worlds** (K = 8 form types; 2 000 draws per instance; 24 instances; seed 0): **A** fixed heterogeneous library (Zipf); **C** local Pólya urn (weights += 1 per realised form); **P** CRR production: next form drawn from the settled block under age weights `q = 0.98` plus a novelty floor `ε = 0.02` (a fading Pólya); **S** one Pólya urn shared by all instances.
**Statistics.** T1 precedence: entropy of the realised form against precedent count (Smolin's signature; F4). T2 cross-instance transfer: Jensen–Shannon divergence between a *naive* instance's first-100 draw distribution and (i) its own prior, (ii) the other instances' realised distribution; T3 reset: erase instance 1's physical record at draw 1 000 and measure the divergence of its next 200 draws from its pre-reset distribution; T4 recency (C against P): log-likelihood gain of a fitted fading kernel over a fitted non-fading urn, original order against shuffled order (PR2's order-gain statistic).
**Predictions.** T1 falls in C, P, S; flat in A. T2: transfer only in S (JSD to others' distribution < 0.02 in S, > 0.2 in A, C, P for the naive instance). T3: C and P reset (post-reset divergence from the pre-reset distribution > 0.2); S does not (< 0.02). T4: P separated from C by order gain > 0 at 2 000 draws; at 300 draws not reliably (de Finetti).
**Meaning.** The Platonic library is decidable against production **only** through non-locality (T2) or non-resettability (T3): a learning library that is local and physically resettable is history-production in other words (F4, confirmed here with an intervention); a shared or non-resettable library predicts cross-instance precedence with no physical channel, which is Sheldrake's claim **[memory: morphic resonance; its tests unreplicated]** and which CRR denies. Either outcome is a DISTINCTION: ND-2's "breaking intervention" column, currently "none for the ontology", gets an entry.

## C4 — REBUS against the CRR temperature in a two-level Gaussian model: state against kernel

**Ingredient.** The seed `m = Σ_j π_j Φ_j` with P2 weights `π_j ∝ exp(βS_j)` or P3 `q^{n−j}`; the deck's "earned weight" (slide 14) as the Kalman gain.
**Beside.** REBUS: relaxation of the precision of high-level priors `Π_p` in a hierarchical Gaussian model; posterior mean `m + K(y − m)`, `K = Π_l/(Π_l + Π_p)`.
**Check 4a (orthogonality).** Jacobian of `(m, K)` with respect to `(Π_p, β)`: **prediction:** `∂K/∂β = 0` and `∂m/∂Π_p = 0` exactly; REBUS's parameter moves `K`, CRR's temperature moves `m`. The deck's equanimity is `K` (REBUS's axis), not `β`.
**Check 4b (episode).** A piecewise-constant world with switches; an agent with the kernel over past occasions and a Kalman update. An "acute phase" of 200 steps with `Π_p` scaled by 0.1. Two post-acute hypotheses: **H-state** (`Π_p` returns, kernel unchanged; the belief has moved) and **H-kernel** (`Π_p` returns and `q` or `β` has changed). Observable: the adaptation time after the *next* switch, 400 steps post-acute. **Prediction:** under H-state post-acute adaptation time equals pre-acute (ratio within 0.05); under H-kernel it differs by > 0.3 for a `q` change 0.98 → 0.90. Must-fail arm: with no acute phase the two hypotheses coincide.
**Meaning.** 4a: REDUNDANT-DOMAIN (the deck's equanimity is the Kalman gain, REBUS's axis). 4b: DISTINCTION, and a falsifiable test: if the therapeutic effect of a psychedelic lives in regeneration (the kernel), post-acute belief-updating *dynamics* (learning-rate adaptation to volatility) change; if it lives in the flattening (the state), only belief *content* changes. The test is runnable on behavioural volatility-learning data collected before and after psilocybin, if such data exist (the REBUS sweep of today reports); CRR shares the "kernel" prediction with any plasticity account, so S2 at most.

## C5 — Self-sorting arrays (Zhang, Goldstein & Levin 2024) through CRR: delayed gratification is surplus

**Ingredient.** `S = C − C*` on a one-dimensional carrier; R3: on a completed monotone traversal `S = 0`, and `S = 2B/σ` with `B` the backward travel.
**World.** Bubble sort run from the "cell's view": each element compares itself with its right neighbour and swaps if out of order, in random order per sweep; a fraction `f ∈ {0, 0.1, 0.3}` of cells is *frozen* (cannot initiate a swap, can be swapped by a neighbour? no: in the reimplementation a frozen cell neither initiates nor accepts, so the array must route around it, which is the paper's "unexpected competency" setting as described in the abstract). Carrier: the number of inversions `e(t)` (sortedness error) after each cell action. `C = Σ|Δe|`, `C* = |e_T − e_0|`, `S = C − C*`, `B = S/2`.
**Predictions.** `f = 0`: `S = 0` exactly (bubble sort never increases inversions). `f > 0`: `S > 0` wherever the array routes around a frozen cell, and `B` equals the paper's "delayed gratification" (the total temporary increase in error) by definition. Must-fail arm: a random-swap null with the same number of swaps has `S` far larger and never sorts.
**Meaning.** DESCR: CRR's surplus is the quantity the authors already measured under another name; CRR adds the identity `S = 2B` (standard) and nothing predictive. The cut and the circle are not applicable (no cyclic carrier). Any further claim (clustering of frozen cells; "algotypes") is outside CRR's objects.
