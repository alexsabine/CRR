How did the seed do?
The four moves
  • Loop. The ring on the left. The bead goes half-way round in one sunspot cycle; the sun’s magnetism flips each time, so the whole ring takes two.
  • Count. How far the sunspot number has travelled this cycle, up and down again.
  • Cut. The flash at each minimum. The cycle is over, and its ball stays on the bench for good.
  • Seed. The cream ring round each ball: the size expected before that cycle was lived. Ball inside the ring, the seed guessed too high.
