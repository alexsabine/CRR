About the living panorama

The round room in the evolution lab has one painting all the way round its wall. This is the same wall, in the same order, with each stretch replaced by a model that runs. Nothing here is a film. Each picture is drawn afresh every frame from numbers the model has just worked out, and the numbers in each label are the model's own.

Only the stretches you can see are run. The rest wait as you left them.

What this leaves out

Scale, first of all. Every model is a small, flat or one-dimensional sketch of something vast, run thousands to billions of times faster than life, with constants chosen so that it can be watched. The pictures are drawn to look like photographs but they are drawings of the model, not photographs of the past. Nobody knows the colour of the early sky. Where and how life began is not settled: the vents and the pools are two serious proposals among several, and neither model shows life beginning. The animals and trees are stand-ins made by rules of movement and growth, not portraits of real species. Four of the five great extinctions are missing, as they are on the painted wall.
