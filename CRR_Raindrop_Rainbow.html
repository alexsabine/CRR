Sunlight enters the droplet as a coherent wavefront, accumulating optical path — C(λ,τ). At each refractive boundary, rupture. What emerges is the regenerated spectrum, each wavelength weighted by exp(C/Ω) where Ω(λ) = 1/πn(λ) differs per colour. The rainbow exists because Ω is wavelength-dependent. The sparkle exists because your eye cannot hold still. C(λ,τ) → δ → R = ∫φ·exp(C/Ω)dτ   ·   Ω(λ) = 1/πn(λ)
