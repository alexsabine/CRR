The Core Insight

The sensory homunculus isn't a distortion — it's what the body actually looks like from inside, weighted by Ω. Hands and face have highest Ω (most degrees of freedom for rupture-regeneration), so they dominate the internal landscape.

Size_internal(part) ∝ Ω(part) · density(δ)

Two Dual Bodies

INTERNAL The body as felt: each region scaled by its Ω-value. High Ω → large representation. This is the proprioceptive CRR body, the body-from-within.

EXTERNAL The body as seen: geometric, φ-proportioned, uniform metric space. This is the visual body encountered in the mirror and in the other.

B_int = ∫ Ω(x) · ρ_CRR(x) dx B_ext = ∫ g(x) dx [geometric metric]

The Mirror Transform

M Maps between internal and external representations. Developmentally constructed through dyadic experience — first seeing the other's body, then one's own in the mirror.

M : Ω-space → geometric space M⁻¹ : geometric → Ω-space

This is a conformal mapping that preserves CRR topology while rescaling magnitudes. It's not learned all at once — it develops through stages:

Developmental Stages

Prenatal (0-birth): Pure Ω-body. No external representation exists. The body IS the homunculus. Mouth dominates (highest prenatal Ω — rooting, sucking).

Dyadic gaze (0-6mo): Seeing the other's face creates the first external anchor. Face-to-face mirroring begins calibrating M for facial regions. The other's face becomes the first geometric reference point.

Hand discovery (3-8mo): Seeing own hands creates direct Ω↔geometric mapping for the highest-Ω body part. The hand is where internal and external first converge in self.

Mirror stage (6-18mo): Lacan's insight, CRR-formalized. Recognizing the mirror image creates M for the whole body. The jubilation of recognition = coherence spike from Ω-geometric alignment. The "Aha!" is literally a CRR δ-moment.

Mature integration: M is fully calibrated. Internal and external representations are dual — each accessible from the other. But the duality is never perfectly resolved; residual Ω-weighting persists in body image.

Body Dysmorphia as CRR

Dysmorphia = failure or distortion of M. The internal Ω-body and external geometric body fail to cohere. Synesthetic body dysmorphia specifically: cross-modal Ω-weighting leaks into visual representation, making the "felt size" override "seen size".

Dysmorphia: M(B_int) ≠ B_ext Synesthetic: Ω_tactile → visual distortion

Why Hands & Face

Hands: highest motor Ω (27 DOF), highest tactile receptor density, most CRR micro-ruptures per second. In the Ω-body, hands are enormous.

Face: highest social Ω (44 muscles for expression), highest dyadic salience, site of primary mirror-recognition. The face is where M is first and most precisely calibrated.

Both are Z₂ structures with bilateral symmetry — the same Ω = 1/π that governs their CRR dynamics.
